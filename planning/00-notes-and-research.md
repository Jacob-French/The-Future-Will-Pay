# Scratchpad: notes & research

## Research that anchors the fiction (real science I'm bending, not inventing)

- **Quantum interest conjecture** (Ford & Roman, Phys. Rev. D 60, 104018, 1999). In quantum field
  theory a pulse of negative energy is allowed, but it must be "repaid" by a later pulse of
  positive energy, and the repayment *exceeds* the loan. The overpayment ("interest") grows the
  longer the loan is held. -> The whole economy of the Draw. The station borrows energy from the
  vacuum, leaving a negative-energy hole, and has spent 91 years rolling the loan over.
- **Gravitational time dilation.** Clocks higher in a gravitational potential run faster (GPS
  satellites gain time). A region of *negative* energy density is gravitationally repulsive: its
  centre is a potential *hill*, not a well. So clocks near the core of the Draw run FAST
  relative to the outside, and "down" near the core points *away* from it. This is the grounding
  for "the further in you go, the faster time moves" and for the city living under a light it
  can't climb toward.
- **Novikov self-consistency principle / Lossev & Novikov, "The Jinn of the time machine"
  (Class. Quantum Grav. 9, 2309, 1992).** A "jinn" is an object whose worldline is a closed loop
  — it has no maker. Self-consistency demands it return to exactly the state it started in, so it
  can't accumulate wear. -> Ione's key is made of keelstone (effectively unwearable). The one
  scratch it has always had is made by the Tear, in front of her, at the end.
- **Chronology protection (Hawking) / Thorne's rule:** you can't use a time machine to reach a time
  before the machine existed. -> The Fold (region of closed timelike curves) was created at the
  rupture, so nothing can loop back to before the rupture. All loops in the story live between
  "hour zero" and the end.
- **Zero-energy universe** (Tryon, "Is the Universe a Vacuum Fluctuation?", Nature 1973). The
  positive energy of matter may be exactly balanced by the negative energy of gravity: the total
  energy of the universe could be zero. The cosmos itself may be a loan against nothing.
  -> The cosmic-horror revelation in the Lace, and its inversion into grace at the end.
- Circulating light: around very compact objects light can orbit (photon sphere). -> The
  "ghosts" in the Carousel are the light of the rupture's first nine minutes, trapped on closed
  paths and replaying forever. Not matter. You can walk through them.
- Scale check: 60 TW for 91 years ≈ 1.7×10^23 J borrowed. With compounded interest the
  Settlement is on the order of 10^26 J — about 250 Chicxulub impacts. Enough to sterilise the
  planet. No black hole needed; just joules.

## Tone touchstones (don't copy, just tune)
- Chernobyl: bureaucratic denial; the cost of lies; ordinary people doing unbearable jobs;
  procedure as liturgy; understatement.
- Children of Time: deep time compressed; a civilisation evolving in isolation over generations;
  awe at scale.
- 1984: the state's language (Continuance-speak), slogans, falsified numbers, rewritten history,
  "calm" as enforced virtue. Mirrored *inside* the station by Holdfast's Stewardship.

## Loose ideas bin (some used, some not)
- "THE FUTURE WILL PAY" — state slogan meaning "future generations will keep the Draw going".
  Literally a debt instrument. Title.
- Emergency placard, Section 9: REMAIN AT YOUR STATION AND AWAIT RELIEF -> Holdfast's scripture.
  "Relief" = the prophesied saviour; at the end, "relieved" = off duty. The Watch is over.
- The Bell: the station's failsafe evacuation hammer strikes once per outside second. Inside,
  stretched by dilation, it's a tide in the floor. As the debt comes due the tide quickens into
  a toll, then a heartbeat. After the seal it beats at exactly one per second: Holdfast's time
  has rejoined the world's.
- Holdfast language: shift = generation; clock off = die; the Drop; the Tanks; greenbread; rats.
- Radio across a time ratio of ~350: her minutes, his weeks. She hears him as bird-chirps;
  he hears her as a slow groan, then the suit plays it back quick and profane.
- Ben's lifetime in a blister, writing on the glass. "MOSTLY ALGAE."
- A figure descending alone in Cas's suit — everyone assumes it's Cas. It's Ione.
- The loop is exactly as long as her leaving. He never experiences her absence.
- The sealed moment has zero net energy — a tiny universe. A man inside it.
