# World bible

## The world outside

**The Commonweal** — a continental state that survived the Long Heat (two centuries of climate
collapse) by building the Draw. Its calendar was reset at the Draw's first ignition: it is now
**Year 91 of Continuance**. The Draw powered the carbon scrubbers that cooled the planet; it
genuinely saved hundreds of millions. That is the moral knot: the thing that saved the world
is also a loan the world can never repay.

**Averna** — the capital megacity. ~600 million people in stacked tiers ("the Stacks") rising
kilometres above old flooded coastline. Under a **Canopy** (a weather shield powered by the Draw),
with desalination towers, vertical farms, the maglev **Lines**, an orbital tether. Never dark:
there have been no visible stars over Averna for three generations. Children sing the Tending
anthem. Plazas have **Quota clocks** displaying the Draw's output in real time.
The Commonweal also *exports* Draw power by superconducting trunk lines to client states who have
become dependent — the Commonweal's geopolitical leverage. Greed is structural: every year the
Quota rises.

**Government**
- **The Directorate** — ruling council.
- **Ministry of Continuance** — runs the Draw and the ideology. Minister: **Ondine Rask**
  (60s, silver, precise, a true believer who has confused the state's survival with the
  species'). Her predecessor-in-deputy **Aldous Crane** was at the station on the night of the
  test and is "unaccounted for".
- **Office of Public Calm** ("the Calm", "Calmers") — secret police whose mandate is preventing
  panic. Calm is a civic virtue; unrest is "a symptom". Colonel **Ansel Gaunt** (softly spoken,
  exhausted, frightening because reasonable).
- **Civil Defence** — the people who actually go into fires. Major **Teodora Lisk**.

**Continuance-speak**: the rupture is "a margin irregularity" / "a Continuance Event". The dead are
"discontinued". Evacuation is "temporary relocation for comfort". Termination of the Draw is
unsayable; the word used is "default".

Slogans: THE FUTURE WILL PAY · WHAT WE BORROW WE KEEP · CALM IS CARE · TEND THE FLAME

## The Draw (Hollowell Draw)

Invented by **Dr Iris Hollowell** (dead 40 years). Official name: Hollowell Draw, Averna Prime.
Workers call it **the Hollow**. Sits in **the Pit**, an excavated crater 14 km across in Averna's
southern industrial belt (the Tallows). Output ~60 terawatts.

**How it works (story physics).** Tensioner rings hold a small region of spacetime below the vacuum
ground state — a negative-energy **Lesion**. The difference is harvested as usable energy. Quantum
interest says the loan must be repaid with interest, growing with time. Hollowell's trick was the
**Rollover**: continuously re-borrowing, feeding part of the output back into the Lattice to push the
repayment forward. Originally the Margin (net output : rollover cost) was 9:1. After 91 years of
compounding and two "Deepenings" to raise output, it was ~1.3:1. The (suppressed) **Margin Review**
projected negative margin within twenty years — after which the Draw would have to be *fed* by the
city just to keep the debt from coming due. A machine that eats the future.

**Hollowell Clause.** Iris Hollowell's last, suppressed paper, *On the Sealing of Debts*, proposed a
catastrophe-only closure: if the Lesion ever ruptured violently enough to create closed timelike
curves, those same loops could be used to fold the core — debt and repayment together — into a
closed moment with no future: a sarcophagus made of time. She built the mechanism into the Crown:
**the Keystone**. It is protected by a physical key held by the Minister of Continuance (the state
insisted engineers could not end the Draw on their own authority). Her paper contains the line
engraved on the Keystone's plate: **"THE SEALING REQUIRES A WITNESS."** Cas has read the paper. He
does not know what the line means. The plate inside the Keystone housing adds a second line:
**"THE WITNESS DOES NOT RETURN."**

**Layout (outside in)**
1. **The Collar** — outer ring: converter halls, trunk-line heads, admin, the gates. Near-normal time.
2. **The Works** — ~4 km of 91 years of additions: rectifier halls, coolant galleries, bioscrubber
   (algae) halls, maintenance shafts, a vertical maze. Time rate 1–3×. Gravity increasingly strange
   ("gravity weather": squalls, shears, lees). After the rupture, fractal **filaments** of the Tear
   thread outward through it, making **blisters**: pockets of wildly fast time.
3. **The Lattice** — concentric tensioner rings of **keelstone** (hyperdense degenerate matter). The
   Shear forms along its inner face.
4. **The Shear** — a thin boundary where time rate jumps from ~2× to ~700× over tens of metres.
   Crossing it without a keel tears a body apart: different parts of you live at different speeds.
5. **The Vault** — a spherical cavity ~9 km across. Its floor is **the Anvil**, a 7 km disc of
   keelstone. **Spindles** (eight maintenance pylons, ~4 km tall) rise from the Anvil to the
   **Rim** (a gantry ring around the Lesion). **The Crown** — control gallery atop Spindle One,
   a designed shelter of keelstone, where the test was run and where the Keystone is.
   Coolant/power **conduits** run down through the Anvil into the Works: Holdfast calls their
   mouths the **Drop wells**.
6. **The Lesion** — the core. Since the rupture its boundary has gone fractal: **the Tear**. Seen
   from Holdfast it is **the Lamp**, a white-violet point in the sky surrounded by **the Lace**, a
   glowing filigree that has been slowly spreading downward for generations.

**Gravity in the Vault.** The Lesion *repels*. On the Anvil, the repulsion adds to Earth's gravity
and the Anvil's own mass: ~1.4 g. Holdfast people are short, dense, strong. Climbing a spindle
toward the Lamp you get **heavier** with every metre. **Lees** — pockets where the pull of keelstone
struts partly cancels the repulsion — are the only way up without a keel. Over the generations the
Lamp's push has been weakening (the debt being called in): founders' records say things were
heavier. Holdfast calls it the **Lightening**.

**Time zones (ratio to outside)** — Works 1–3 · Teo's Lee at the Shear edge ~2 · Holdfast
(Anvil) ~250,000 at founding, ~700 at Cas's arrival · Carousel ~ Holdfast×550 · the Lace and
Crown: undefined; loops.

**The ratio is falling.** As the debt comes due, the hill shrinks and the inside's time rate sinks
toward the outside's. Model: R(t) ≈ 250,000·e^(−t/5.6h). Holdfast age at Cas's arrival (outside hour
33): ~168 years. Remaining Holdfast time if nothing is done: a few months at most, and the Lace will
reach the Rim sooner — when it touches, the Settlement comes.

**The Settlement** — the repayment, ~10^26 J, released at once. Predicted by Cas at outside hour
~55–70. It would erase Averna in the first second and sterilise the planet within the hour.

**The Fold and the rules of time (one model for everything).** Spacetime is a single self-consistent
block. Nothing is ever "reset"; nothing can be changed; loops are shapes, not replays.
1. Closed timelike curves exist only in the Fold (the Lace, the Crown), which formed at the rupture — so no loop
   reaches before hour zero.
2. Everything that happens there is self-consistent (Novikov). Objects can have closed worldlines ("jinn") but
   must come back exactly as they left — hence a keelstone key.
3. The Carousel is *not* a time loop for people: a gallery closed on itself in space, full of trapped light from
   the rupture's first nine minutes. Visitors live forward (age, remember); the ghosts repeat. Repetition is
   horror only for the observer.
4. The Hollowell seal folds the core into a loop of zero length: one instant whose future is itself. Its contents
   can't differ from themselves, so it is stable for ever. The witness inside lives that instant once.

**Keel suits** (Metric-Stabilised Exosuit, Type 4) — three prototypes from the Tallow Works lab,
built for a cancelled Lattice maintenance programme. Exoskeleton + life support + the **keel**: a
keelstone rotor spun to enormous speed whose frame-dragging smooths the local metric in a bubble about
a metre across. Mechanical; spins down over days; doesn't need electronics once spun. Two people
pressed close can share one bubble. Suit electronics (radio, displays, recorder) degrade in steep time
gradients (a chip whose ends live at different speeds can't keep its clocks together).

**Radio across ratios.** Signals from slower regions arrive stretched (slowed, deepened); from faster
regions, compressed. The suits buffer and re-time them. Between Teo (R≈2) and Cas in Holdfast
(R≈700): ×350. Her one-minute message takes ~6 hours to arrive; his ten-minute message reaches
her as a 2-second chirp. Ben fitted relay pucks; the conduit acts as a waveguide.

## Holdfast

**Founding.** At the rupture, ~1,900 people were in the Vault: the night shift of the Lattice and Anvil
crews, the Crown crew (who got down the Spindle One lift before it failed), and the Ministry
delegation there to film the test, led by Deputy Minister **Aldous Crane**. The Shear formed around
them. Those who tried to go down through the conduits died horribly. Crane took command and gave
them a story: the Lamp was *Kindled*, not ruptured; the Outside burned; we are the Watch; we
**remain at our station and await relief** (Section 9 of the station emergency placard, now scripture).
The test is not mentioned in any Holdfast history.

**Now (~168 years, ~7 "shifts").** ~31,000 people. Built of scaffolding, cargo frames, keelstone
off-cuts; stacked around the spindle bases; the great **Commons** (former assembly bay). Diet:
algae (**greenbread**), fungus grown on old insulation, crickets, and rats (Holdfast's "other
citizens"; bred, named, eaten, loved). The dead go to the Tanks. Light: the Lamp; they shutter
windows to make night. Their day is a "round" of three shifts.

**Language.** shift = generation · clock on / clock off = born / die · on watch = alive · the Drop =
the conduits, and death · "Lamp keep you" · "Drop take it" · Relief = the prophesied saviour who will
come up from below and end the Watch · riggers climb spindles · Tollers count Bells.

**The Bell.** The station's failsafe hammer ("the Dead Man's Hammer", flywheel-driven) strikes once per
outside second. Through the Anvil it arrives in Holdfast stretched: in the founders' time a tide in the
floor lasting half a day, every few days. Now a two-minute swell every ~12 minutes. Holdfast's calendar
is the Bell count. The count is the age of the world: ~119,000 Bells. The **Quickening** — the Bells
coming faster — is officially a blessing: the Relief draws near.

**The Stewardship.** Ruled by **First Steward Halloran Crane** (60s), sixth-generation descendant of
Aldous. He knows the Quickening means the end: the Tollers report to him, and he publishes a
falsified **Public Tally**. Not a monster: he believes panic would kill everyone before the Lamp does
and wants them to die calm. The Steward's House sits at the base of Spindle One. Ascent is forbidden.
Guards (Wardens) watch the Drop wells for jumpers.

**The Tollers.** Keepers of the Bell count in Toller's Tower. The Tally family descend from **Ruth Tally**,
the shift engineer who ran the test under the Deputy Minister's orders. Ruth's true logbook — **the
Tally Log** — has been secretly kept for seven generations. Head Toller: **Perpetua Tally** (80s,
Ione's grandmother, sharp, very funny, dying slowly and uninterested in discussing it).

## Characters

**Casimir "Cas" Hale** (47). Field physicist, the Draw's emergency Standby. Fifteen years ago, a junior
member of the Margin Review. He co-wrote the warning; when the Ministry rewrote the conclusion, he
signed the revised report. Nobody made him. He was "rewarded" with Standby: a flat near the Pit, a
salary, a phone that never rang. Divorced (Sabine). Daughter **Nell** (10), who collects stars she's
never seen — star charts, glow stickers, names of constellations. He is a man who has not said a true
thing out loud in fifteen years. He goes in intending to die: it would be tidy. Dry, precise, gentle,
funny in a tired way. Arc: from atonement-by-death (dread; "nothing I do matters except the paying")
to a freely chosen moment (meaning). Ends as the Witness: inside the sealed moment, for ever, once.

**Major Teodora "Teo" Lisk** (52). Civil Defence. Veteran of the Tessarine floods and the Kovač stack
fire. Profane, practical, tender underneath, mother of two grown sons. Secretly given orders by
Gaunt to make sure Hale "restores Continuance" — i.e. doesn't end the Draw. Throws the orders away in
the Works. Gives Cas her keel at the Shear and is left in a Lee to wait. Survives. "Guilt's a lousy
reason, Hale. Die for something useful or don't bother."

**Benedek "Ben" Farr** (26). Suit engineer who helped build the keels. Talks when frightened, which is
always. Engaged to Jonah, a nurse. Brilliant. Trapped in a blister in the Works, lives eleven years
in about two hours of theirs, maps the Tear's filaments on the glass, and dies of the blister's final
acceleration in front of them. His map gets Cas through.

**Ione Tally** (born 28 Holdfast-years ago; body age ~31). Toller-born rigger and salvager. At 17 she
ascended alone, was caught in the Carousel for three subjective years, and came back after two
Holdfast days with a grey streak in her hair and a keelstone key she found in the loop. Branded
**Unwatched** (heretic); called "Lamp-mad Ione". In the Carousel she watched the light-ghosts of the
rupture's first nine minutes ~175,000 times: she has seen what really happened in the Crown, knows every
move of the console, and knows the Keystone lock her key fits. Since then she has watched the Drop wells,
because once, from the Carousel, she glimpsed a figure in a strange suit coming down alone. Calm,
exact, patient, dry; counts things; says what's about to happen because she knows how moments go;
tender with children; laughs at absurdity; timeless and a little unreachable. Arc: from living outside
time (a ghost among people, waiting) back into linear time — an ordinary unknown future. Ends walking
out under the stars.

**Halloran Crane** (60s) — First Steward. See above. At the end: "Relief has come. We are relieved."

**Pim** (11) — Holdfast rat-boy, Ione's shadow, the city's cheerful news service.

**Warden Osk** — Stewardship guard, big, slow-spoken, decent; changes sides.

**Ruth Tally** — prologue POV. Shift engineer. Said no, then did it anyway, then tried to undo it.

**Aldous Crane** — Deputy Minister at the test. Ordered the interlocks off for the anniversary footage.
Held the Keystone key and would not hand it over; the key fell into the Tear when the Crown gantry
buckled. Founded Holdfast on a lie.

**Nell Hale** (10), **Sabine** (ex-wife) — outside.

## Time model cheat-sheet (added while writing — use these numbers)
- R(t) = 250,000·e^(−t/5.6h) (t = outside hours). Holdfast year Y = 168.5·(1 − e^(−t/5.6)).
- Handy identity: **R = 250,000 × (1 − Y/168.5)**. Bell interval (in Holdfast seconds) = R.
- Y 0: Bell every ~3 rounds · Y 50 (t≈2h, Bell ~7,100): ~2 rounds · Y 108: ~1/round ·
  Y 148 (Crane made Steward): ~3/round · Y 155 (Seren's Drop, t≈14h): ~4/round ·
  Y 157 (Ione's Carousel, t≈15h) · Y 160: ~7/round · Y 165: ~17/round ·
  Y 168 (Cas arrives, t≈33.2h, Bell ~119,500): every ~12 min, ~116/round.
- Outside Hours on the Tollers' column: first took ~26 years, second ~22; the current one ~4 months.
- Left, if nothing is done: about half a Holdfast year; the Lace reaches the Rim sooner (weeks).
- The first Crown letter "twenty years" ≈ 40 outside minutes; Ruth's last (Year 50) ≈ 2 hours.

## v2 additions (editorial pass — see notes.md)
- **Where gradients sit.** Time behaves like pressure and matter resists it. In open air a fast
  region thins out smoothly with distance (roughly as the square). Where fast meets slow across
  something solid, the whole difference ("the drop") bunches up inside the solid; denser holds more;
  keelstone holds most (the Lattice is a dam for time). A thread in an open room = a local fast patch.
  A thread in a sealed room = a **blister**: the whole room at the thread's speed, the drop in its walls.
  Opening a door in a blister puts the whole drop through the doorway, then sweeps out as a front.
  The Vault is one giant blister; the Shear is the drop sitting in the air of the conduits (holes in the dam).
- **Keelstone** is ~10× the density of lead (11 kg rotor = dinner-plate disc 2 mm thick; key ≈ 300 g).
- **Prologue:** 100 = 60 TW; 130 = 78 TW; Crane wants a round **80 TW**, which needs 135.
- **Key timings:** Kindling Round = Bell 121,944 (round 19); last Bell on the climb 123,714; seal /
  last Log Bell 123,716 (outside hour ~34.4); Lace meets Rim ≈ round 66 ≈ outside hour 36.
  Teo's chair runs ×1.9; one Holdfast round ≈ 4 of Teo's minutes.
