# Step 7 — Final revision (the written story)

Read back the finished text against the plan, the world bible and itself. What changed:

## Logic and continuity fixes
- **Time direction on the spindle.** The draft had the city below "blinking" faster as they climbed.
  Backwards: higher = faster time, so from above the city should *slow* and freeze. Rewrote Ch.12 so
  the shutter-wave crawls, the smoke stands up "like wire", the city becomes a photograph — and the
  last shutter of morning rising at the end of the chapter.
- **One time model everywhere.** Recomputed every Holdfast date from R = 250,000 × (1 − Y/168.5):
  Ruth's letters (Year 20 ≈ 40 minutes outside; Year 50 ≈ 2 hours), the Tollers' Outside Hours (the
  first took 26 years), Crane's childhood Bell rates, Seren's Drop (≈19 outside hours before Cas
  arrived, while he was learning to walk in a keel suit), the festival (Kindling Round, Bell ~121,640,
  not a round 120,000 that would have fallen too early), the last Bell counted (122,931 on the climb;
  122,988 in the Log).
- **Outside timeline.** Spin-up now starts at 13:00 and finishes at 03:00; entry at hour ~25.5, so the
  Works journey (≈12 subjective hours at ratio 1.5–2) lands Cas in Holdfast at hour 33:11 as the Tollers
  say. Hammer counts in Ch.1 corrected to match the clock.
- **Keel spin-down.** A 5–6 day rotor couldn't have survived four Holdfast weeks. Now the rotor barely
  loses spin at rest and runs down by *working* against gradients — consistent with Teo's cracked one
  dying in hours, and Teo's good one dropping "a semitone" after the Shear and more after each storm.
- **Ben's grease pencil** was inside the blister, so Teo couldn't have picked it up. She now pockets the
  black marker Cas wrote with on their side of the glass. The names on her window are in marker.
- **Ben's birthdays** ("5") didn't match his age at that point; now 8.
- **Teo's messages** vs the Interlude: removed a Gaunt phone call she couldn't yet have had; the lee
  moved "twenty" (not ten) minutes before her second message.
- **Ione's wrist** was in a sling for a climb — now a splint, which she re-breaks in the Breach.
- **The woman on the gantry**: made her position consistent across Ch.12, 14 and 16 (far end of the
  gantry by Spindle One's wall, where the seam is left open).
- **The seal instant.** The Coda had Ione's arm raised at the instant, but the figure seen from the Rim
  lowers its arm and goes on down in Lamp-light, so the seal must come a few steps later. The Coda now
  holds her light going down, with the raised hand a moment before, and "He saw." Ione's uncertainty
  in Ch.18 is whether his eyes were on her or the needle, which the Coda quietly answers.
- **Ruth's glimpse** now says *kneeling where she knelt*, matching the Coda. Added Ruth's Year 31
  entry to the Log in Ch.8 so the man in grey with the knotted cord is planted before Ch.17 pays it off.
- Small facts: Ben's age at the First Deepening, the address being fifteen (not eighteen) hours late,
  a Canopy anachronism in Cas's rain memory, Teo's suit description in Ch.18, the seam direction when
  Ione leaves the Carousel, "two days after Continuance Day".
