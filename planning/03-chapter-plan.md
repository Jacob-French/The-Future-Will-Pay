## Threads to track across chapters (plant → pay off)
- **The Bell**: hammer across Averna (1) → tide in the floor (6) → Toller count / Quickening (8) →
  last Bell heard on the climb (12) → one per second, a heartbeat (18).
- **The key**: falls in Prologue → Ione's key seen (8) → her story of finding it (12) → Carousel ghost
  of Crane pocketing the original (14) → the scratch (16) → given to her younger self (16).
- **The figure descending in a keel suit**: Ione mentions she's waited at the wells because she once
  saw someone in a strange suit (6/9) → seen together in the Lace (15) → it's her (17).
- **"The sealing requires a witness"**: Cas quotes Hollowell (1) → tells Ione (8) → plate (16).
- **Placard / Relief**: poster in the Collar (3) → scripture (7) → "We are relieved" (18).
- **Nell's stars**: her glow-stars and star charts (2) → Cas describes the Lamp/Lace to her in
  recordings (9) → stars over dark Averna (19) → Ione gives Nell a star name / Nell teaches Ione (21).
- **Teo's messages**: promised at the Shear (5) → first (9) → second (11) → her side (I) → alive (19).
- **Ben's map**: drawn on glass (4) → Cas's sketchbook copy guides him through the Shear (5) and the
  Lace filaments agree with it (15).
- **The man at the Keystone who isn't there** (Prologue) ↔ Cas sees Ruth's ghost at the console (16) ↔
  Coda.
- **The Tally Log** (8) → carried down by Ione (18) → public (21).
- **Weight**: 1.4 g (6) → Lightening (8) → crushing on the climb (12–15) → ordinary (18) → the Holdfast
  people feel they're floating outside (19).
- **"Once"**: Ione's line about closed loops (12) → Cas's choice (17) → Coda title.
